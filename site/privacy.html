Stars Replay privacy

Stars Replay is a browser extension that records your moves while you solve a Stars or Mosaic puzzle on inkwellgames.com, so you can replay the solve or share it as a link.

What it collects

While you are recording, the extension watches the puzzle board on the page and stores the sequence of moves (X marks, stars, highlights, hints, checks, undos, pauses) with timestamps. That is the recording. It is stored in your browser's extension storage, on your device.

What it sends

Nothing, on its own. The extension makes no network requests. A recording leaves your device only when you choose to share it: the share link contains the whole recording encoded in the URL, and opening that link renders it on this site. Share links are not stored anywhere by this site; the preview image for a link is generated from the link itself when a page or chat app asks for it.

What it can see

The extension runs only on inkwellgames.com pages. It reads the puzzle board and the game's buttons. It does not read your account, your password, cookies, or anything on other sites.

Analytics on this site

This website (not the extension) uses anonymous page-view analytics shared with the rest of drewhoover.com. No account or personal information is involved.

Deleting data

Delete recordings from the extension's popup, or remove the extension, which clears its storage. A link you have already shared keeps working, because the recording lives inside the link.
